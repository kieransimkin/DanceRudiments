What you are seeing

These are abstract object trajectories, not motion-captured dancers. The faint line is the complete path. The moving dot is an exact integer-pip C++ sample. Depth uses an oblique projection; the three curves show X, Y and Z.

Impact kernels anticipate hits; the spring starts at the hit and rings afterwards. Phase-driven paths respond through speed and radius rather than peaking at each event. Missing bass/backbeat/high-percussion lanes use declared kick fallbacks. Overlaps can change the combined peak. The position grid is 1/64 of a quarter-note beat; audio events may fall between pips.

What you are hearing

A newly synthesised drum kit and simple pitched bass cues. No original Amen recording, commercial samples or downloaded music. The Amen is a quantised four-bar structural study, not the original drummer’s groove or timbre.

Each genre has many valid rhythms. These are documented examples and original variants—not a genre classifier. Tempo is adjustable; preset BPMs are audition suggestions.
